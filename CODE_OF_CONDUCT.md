# Code of Conduct

**TL;DR: be kind, be direct, assume good faith.**

In the interest of an open and welcoming project, everyone who takes part in
pikopod's community spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and
any affiliated channels, agrees to follow this code of conduct.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of background, gender, gender identity and expression, age, sexual
orientation, disability, physical appearance, body size, race, ethnicity,
religion or lack thereof, level of experience, or any other dimension of
diversity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. Review comments are about
  the code, not the person who wrote it.
- Assume good faith. Most disagreements are a missing piece of context rather
  than bad intent.
- Be direct and specific. "This breaks the fail-open contract because X" helps.
  "This is wrong" does not.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologize to those affected, and learn from
  the experience.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment of any kind, public or private.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Insults, personal attacks, or derogatory comments.
- Sexist, racist, or otherwise exclusionary jokes or language.
- Trolling or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address,
  without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or pull requests.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces, and also applies when an
individual is officially representing the project in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it to
the maintainers, privately, via a [GitHub Security Advisory](https://github.com/pikopod/pikopod/security/advisories/new).

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. We will
respect the privacy and security of the reporter, and we will not take action
against someone for reporting in good faith.

If a report concerns a maintainer, say so in your message. We will arrange for
someone not involved to handle it.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code of conduct,
and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other
contributions that do not align with it.

Consequences scale with the behavior:

1. **Correction.** A private written warning explaining what was inappropriate
   and why, possibly with a request for a public apology.
2. **Warning.** A warning with consequences for continued behavior, including a
   period of no interaction with those involved.
3. **Temporary ban.** A temporary ban from any interaction with the project.
4. **Permanent ban.** A permanent ban from any public interaction with the
   project.
